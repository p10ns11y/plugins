## What this plugin is / is not

- **Is:** an agent skill/procedure for foggy finance and life decisions under uncertainty.
- **Is not:** a republication of Venix’s article prose, or financial advice.
- The mathematical ideas (EV, Bayes’ theorem, multiplicative ruin, Kelly criterion)
  are classical public mathematics and long predate that article.

When emitting `/uncertainty-laws` results, prefer a short credit line when the
four-law sequence is used.
