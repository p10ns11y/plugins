On looping an agent until the tool passes, he said he is "sacrificing productivity for higher quality and at some point that's got to give way. But I haven't found the end point of that yet" (0:17:35).

Video times (h:mm:ss): the chain 0:17:00–0:23:00, CRAP thresholds 0:32:00–0:33:00, TDD not imposed on agents 0:34:00–0:35:00, specifier 0:20:24, hardener 0:21:57, human and agent CRAP limits 0:32:52–0:32:58.

CRAP is by Alberto Savoia and Bob Evans (crap4j, 2007): `CRAP(m) = comp(m)^2 * (1 - cov(m))^3 + comp(m)`. The original used basis-path coverage. This plugin uses line coverage.

The 0.95 mutation minimum is our default. He said the hardener is "going to have 100% coverage" (0:21:57).
