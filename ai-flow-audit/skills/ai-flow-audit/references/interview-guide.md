# Interview guide — frontline first

Draft protocol. Placeholders only. No customer data in the template. No one in the room authorizes pay, send, delete, merge, publish, or legal submit.

## Purpose

A short structured talk that shows where real work diverges from the AI story. Interview people who do the work before people who sponsor it. Record evidence IDs and paraphrased quotes.

| Segment | Minutes | Outcome |
|---|---:|---|
| Consent, scope, role, process map | 3–5 | Safe frame; trigger and outcome named |
| Walk one recent case | 8–12 | Tools, handoffs, decisions, waits |
| Core questions | 10–15 | Ignore points, promises, workarounds, rework |
| Myth / confusion capture | 3–5 | One card each if supported |
| Close and verify | 2–3 | Correct notes; next evidence owner |

## Who to interview, in order

1. **Frontline operator(s)** who run the process (newer and experienced if you can).
2. **Manager / process owner** who sees exceptions.
3. **Sponsor** who approved the AI spend or outcome.
4. Optional: support, security, finance, or system owner only for a specific gap.

Use the same core questions across roles so claims can be compared.

## Prep

- Confirm one process, trigger, outcome, sample window, ship date, and stop date.
- Sketch trigger → tools → human decisions → handoffs → output → rework.
- Bring ledger evidence IDs. Do not lead with a maturity label.
- Ask permission for notes and a closing paraphrase check.
- Never request secrets, credentials, or raw customer prompts.
- Rule: evidence gathering, not performance review. Roles replace names in drafts.

## Question bank

### Walk the work

- Walk the last ordinary case from trigger to done. What did you touch? Where did you wait?
- Which step is supposed to use AI? What do you actually do?
- Who checks, edits, approves, merges, sends, pays, or handles exceptions?

### Core questions

1. Where do you ignore the AI on purpose? Why is that safer or faster?
2. What did leadership promise that did not show up in daily work?
3. What workaround would embarrass you if filmed? How often? Who fixes it?
4. What would you delete tomorrow if allowed?
5. When did AI output create rework lately? What was wrong? Who caught it? How long to recover?

### Neutral probes

- One recent date or example?
- How often in a normal week? How many minutes each time?
- Tool spend, delay, missed work, or another person's time?
- Observed, estimated, or belief?
- What must stay human-approved?

### Close

- What did I get too certain?
- Next evidence: time, money, quote, or autopsy?
- May I use this paraphrase with your role only?

## After each interview

Write one myth candidate, one confusion candidate, one missing evidence item. None of these are findings until the evidence bar is met.
