# EVA emptiness — English procedure

Expand **only if** the formal `SKILL.md` is ambiguous for the current step.  
Do not dual-load with the skill body by default.

## `disprove_with`

Cheapest check that would prove the #1 assumption wrong. Write it on the Card after listing assumptions and ranking blast radius. If you cannot name assumptions → Ask; do not Act.

## Pathways (Simulate, emptiness med|high)

Each prior fork returns a short pathway:

1. Near-term action  
2. When it fails (tipping)  
3. Which signpost forces **switch**  

Prefer one line on “under which futures this breaks” over long prose.

## Score (A8 extended)

Prefer pathways that:

- Still meet essential Correctness/Effectiveness under more emptiness (**RobustSatisficing**)  
- Keep at least one strong alternate live (**OptionPreserve**)  

Penalize winners that look good only under the nominal prior.

## ActOrAsk

| Choice | Plain meaning |
|--------|----------------|
| continue | Do the next step of `pathway_active` |
| switch | Pick another pathway; Outer re-ORIENT (not silent Act) |
| Ask | Human decides (auth, contradiction, or switch needs ack) |

Under no data: be robust and humble. Certainty without evidence is free and usually wrong.
