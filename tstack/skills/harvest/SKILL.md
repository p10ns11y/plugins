# harvest

1. Evidence is a link, a sent-draft id, a merged SHA or a signpost. No evidence, no row.
2. Append one row to LOG.md: date, Do, result, evidence. Money, health and family figures stay out of public files.
3. Log the win: `premflow win <text>`. Close the task: `premflow task done <n>`.
4. Store the result in **pulse-memory**. **mission-map** marks the Do as Done.
